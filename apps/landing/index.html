It listens to one side of the call

On your computer the call audio is the customer and your microphone is you. Only the customer's track can bring up a card, so the app never answers your own sentences back to you.

Around the call

  1. Before you dial

    Your Zoho meeting, already open

    Connect your own Zoho CRM account. Pick an upcoming meeting and the app writes a short brief: the objective, who is attending, open points and the next step. Nothing is attached to the call until you confirm it, and a brief can be written by hand without Zoho.

  2. When the playbook has no answer

    It says so instead of guessing

    An answer from outside your playbook is marked "Not in playbook, check before promising". If your company switches those answers off, you get a holding line to say, and your sales manager gets the question by email. The manager's reply can then go into the playbook for the next call.

  3. After you hang up

    You approve every update before it leaves

    The app suggests what to record: a meeting, a lead, a contact change, a deal note, a follow-up email or a WhatsApp message. Each one waits for your tick and shows you what will be saved or sent.

What it will not do

  • Bring up a card because of something you said.
  • Write to Zoho, send an email or send a WhatsApp message without your approval.
  • Change a deal's stage or amount in Zoho.
  • Present a guess as if it came from your playbook.
  • Keep a copy of your WhatsApp conversations.
