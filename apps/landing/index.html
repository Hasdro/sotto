It listens to one side of the call

On your computer the call audio is the customer and your microphone is you. Only the customer's track can bring up a card, so the app never answers your own sentences back to you.

When the playbook has no answer, it says so

An answer from outside your playbook is marked "Not in playbook, check before promising". If your company turns those off, you get a holding line to say, and your sales manager gets the question by email. Their reply can go into the playbook for the next call.

What it will not do

You stay in charge of the call and of your CRM. These are rules in the product, not settings someone can forget.

  • Bring up a card because of something you said.
  • Write to Zoho, send an email or send a WhatsApp message without your approval.
  • Change a deal's stage or amount in Zoho.
  • Present a guess as if it came from your playbook.
  • Keep a copy of your WhatsApp conversations.
