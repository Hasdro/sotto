## Mascot / character art [O]
- A rounded, cream-coloured, soft-lit 3D-rendered creature on a transparent background, shown at about 56px in the header and larger in avatar galleries.
- Qualities that matter: soft ambient lighting, no outlines, matte surfaces, a gentle off-white/cream tone that sits comfortably on #fcfcfc, round volumes.
- Users can restyle the character (tips in the conversation mention outfits and colour shifts [O]), so the avatar slot is a themeable image, not a fixed logo.
- To inherit the idea: give your product a single soft, round, matte, softly lit character or object, rendered with no strokes, in warm off-white. Do not draw this particular character.

## Iconography [V geometry, O style]
- Each rail icon is an SVG with `viewBox="0 0 320 320"` rendered at 32–34px, filled paths (no stroke attribute), colour #111112.
- Visual style: monoline outline glyphs with rounded ends, generous interior space, about 1.5–2px apparent stroke at 24px, geometric and friendly (chat bubble, magnifier, notebook, lightbulb, checkbox, four-shape grid, plus-in-square, download tray, hamburger lines) [O].
- Inline icons in rows and menus are about 16px with the same weight [O].
- Brand glyphs (e.g. GitHub) sit on a white rounded-square tile about 40px, radius about 10px, inside the gray link card [O].
- Active/hover treatment is a gray circle behind the icon, not a colour change on the glyph [O].

## Illustration and imagery [O]
- Product mock-ups appear inside message bubbles: a phone frame with a soft pale-blue halo/tint behind it, rounded corners matching the bubble radius, and no hard shadow.
- Inside the mock-up, a dark-blue-to-black card shows dashboard tiles with bright green/white numerals — the only high-contrast, saturated moment in the shell. It is user-facing content, not chrome.
- Round placeholder discs (44px, #f3f4f5-ish) stand in for list icons until artwork loads.

## Graphic motifs
- Circles (hover backdrops, list icons, avatar, unread dots).
- Pills (controls).
- One-corner-sharper rounded rectangles (bubble tail).
- Frosted translucent panes layered over content.
- Hairlines `rgba(0,0,0,.1)` only on structural dividers (rail, search field, sheet header).

## Do / Don't
- Do keep graphics matte, rounded, softly lit, and sparing.
- Do give character art room (a quiet header position, 56px or larger).
- Don't add outlines, hard drop shadows, glossy highlights, or neon gradients.
- Don't mix filled and heavily stroked icon sets; keep a single monoline family.
