AGN Sales Shield: three logo directions

Standalone symbols, no letters. Each is shown at 512, 32 and 16 px on light and dark, as the app icon, and as the one-colour menu-bar icon macOS tints itself. The owner picked direction c (12 Nov 2026); it is wired into the apps, installers, tray and landing page.

aHandset shield

A handset bent into the outline of a shield: the earpiece and mouthpiece are its shoulders, the grip is the point. Open at the top, so it still reads as a phone.

bHandset and check

A plain handset with one warm check beside the earpiece: the call is covered. The most literal of the three; the check carries the signal colour.

cShield monogram

A solid shield with the handset cut out of it. Heaviest at small sizes and the easiest to spot in a tray, but the most conventional.

Chosen: c, Shield monogram (owner, 12 Nov 2026). Rasters come from export.mjs c.
