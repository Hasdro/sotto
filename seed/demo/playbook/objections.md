# Objections (demo, fictional)

## Q: It's too expensive.
- Short answer (≤ 25 words, what the agent can say): I understand. Is it the total price or the monthly payments that worry you? The plan spreads ninety percent over four years.
- Details (for the agent's eyes): Move the conversation to the payment plan and smaller units.
- Follow-up question to ask: What budget did you have in mind?
- Tags: expensive, price, budget, cost, too much

## Q: Another company quoted us less.
- Short answer (≤ 25 words, what the agent can say): Fair point. What does their quote include? Let's compare both line by line, including service charges and handover date.
- Details (for the agent's eyes): Ask about size, floor, view, fees and developer track record.
- Follow-up question to ask: Can you share their offer so we compare properly?
- Tags: competitor, cheaper, quote, quoted, less, another company, other offer

## Q: I need to think about it.
- Short answer (≤ 25 words, what the agent can say): Of course. What is the one thing you'd want to be sure about before deciding?
- Details (for the agent's eyes): Surface the real concern; offer a viewing to make it concrete.
- Follow-up question to ask: Shall we book a viewing so you can decide with all the facts?
- Tags: think, decide, later, not sure, consider

## Q: Just send me the details on WhatsApp.
- Short answer (≤ 25 words, what the agent can say): Happy to. So I send the right units, is this for living or investment, and which size?
- Details (for the agent's eyes): Agree, but qualify first so the follow-up is relevant.
- Follow-up question to ask: Can I call you back after you've looked, say tomorrow evening?
- Tags: whatsapp, send, details, brochure, email

## Q: I'm not interested.
- Short answer (≤ 25 words, what the agent can say): No problem. May I ask if it's the timing or the area? Then I won't call about the wrong things.
- Details (for the agent's eyes): Respect it. One question, then close politely.
- Follow-up question to ask: Is it alright if I check back in a few months?
- Tags: not interested, no thanks, stop

## Q: Off-plan is risky.
- Short answer (≤ 25 words, what the agent can say): A fair worry. Your payments go into a government-regulated escrow account, released only as construction progresses.
- Details (for the agent's eyes): Escrow is mandatory for off-plan sales. Developer has delivered on time before (demo fact).
- Follow-up question to ask: Would it help to see the construction progress report?
- Tags: risk, risky, off plan, safe, trust, escrow
